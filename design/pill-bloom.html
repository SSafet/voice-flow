Rules stolen from the video

  • Anchored, never floating. Every card grows out of the pill and collapses back into it — the video's cards bloom from their bar chip. No second window, ever.
  • One bloom at a time. Opening a chip's card closes the previous one, like the rice closes the music card when the battery card opens.
  • Transient by contract. Esc / click-away / picking another chip always returns to the pill. "More space" is something you summon, not something you keep.
  • The dots never move. Strip and every bloom keep the session dots in the bottom band of the one shape — same invariant as today.
  • Hero fact, quiet rest. Each card leads with its one number or line big (the rice's 100% / 9.3° / 11:56) — the question being asked, the queue count, the playback position.
  • Chips only when alive. A subsystem with nothing going on shows no chip — the strip is a status readout, not a menu.
