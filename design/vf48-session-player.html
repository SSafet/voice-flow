2 · Consumption — the two-tier ring, and what "consumed" means

The new dot grammar — 1 consumed (grey, still selectable), 2 active, 3 unread (static amber — no pulse), 4 asking (pulse reserved for questions). Slack: bold for unread, red only for direct address. Stories: seen ring greys, stays tappable.

Interrupted mid-stack — stopping is not consuming: the stack keeps an in-progress position and resumes there next time. Pocket Casts in-progress episodes; YouTube's resume bar.

The consumption rule (podcast three-state, extended to text): a push is consumed when its text was actually rendered in the grown view (Telegram viewport rule — a receipt flash never consumes) OR when TTS finished speaking it (~95%). Partial listening leaves an in-progress resume position. A consumed session's dot greys but keeps its number and stays selectable; its stack stays browsable in the Agents panel forever (Visual Voicemail: the row never moves). A new push fully re-lights the session. Asks are NEVER consumed by reading or listening — they pulse until answered. Consumption is strictly user-local: agents never see "seen", only "delivered".

5 · Open decisions — needs your call (no product precedent)

1 · What consumes on display? Growing a stack shows all active pushes at once. Recommend: whatever was actually rendered is consumed (Telegram viewport rule) — for stacks that fit, that's the whole stack; a receipt flash or the 5s re-preview of an already-seen stack consumes nothing new. Alternative: only the newest push, or an explicit consume chord.
2 · Does listening to the CLEANED text count as seeing the verbatim one? Recommend: yes for the unread ring (the meaning was delivered); the ✦ marks stay visible in the panel so you can always audit what was swapped.
3 · Cross-session auto-advance. When a stack finishes playing, continue into the next unread session's audio? Zero precedent anywhere. Recommend: no — end tone + "next: … — ⌃⌥N" receipt; you advance with ⌃⌥. when you want it. (A "play everything" gesture could be F8 on the picker.)
4 · Chord budget. The map adds ⌃⌥0, ⌃⌥, / ⌃⌥. and ⌃⌥V. Is that acceptable, or should any be dropped/merged? Global focus-free triage has no product precedent — this vocabulary is ours to define.
5 · Now Playing registration. Free media keys + AirPods + Control Center while VF speaks, but it steals those keys from your music. Recommend: ship as a Settings toggle, off by default.
6 · Six-slot aging. When a 7th session arrives, which dot yields? Recommend: the oldest fully-consumed, fully-silent session drops off the picker (still in the panel); numbers of surviving sessions never shift mid-day.
7 · Progress representation — RESOLVED (your 12:56 capture): the hairline is gone everywhere; on the strip the waveform is the progress bar and the scrubber (lit = played, drag to seek), the label carries "2/3" for the stack; the collapsed pill shows no progress at all.
8 · Panel progress sliver. The in-progress resume sliver on an Agents row (journey step 6) technically violates panel remark #4 ("no decorations — unread = the row simply reads bright"). It is data, not decoration — but it's your rule to bend or keep.

6 · Engineering notes (for the implementation ticket)

· Seekability: read-aloud of a stack starts from full known text → generate as cached audio (TTSController already caches finished streams and seeks cached WAVs; `seek(to:)` is cache-only today). Sentence boundaries → frame offsets gives exact sentence skips. Live streamed replies stay skip-limited (no cache) — the player shows pause/stop only there.

· Waveform: bars are the RMS amplitude envelope of the cached WAV (downsampled to ~16–24 buckets — exactly how WhatsApp draws voice notes), so the strip shows the real shape of the speech. Lit bars = played fraction; drag = `seek(to:)`; the 2 bars at the playhead animate while playing. Live streams draw the envelope of what has already been fed and mark the strip non-draggable.

· Speed: baked into generation per request. The cycling chip re-generates from the current sentence at the new speed — cheap because speech is already sentence-chunked (AgentReplySpeaker).

· State: SessionPush already carries seen/spoken/done — the three-state model extends `spoken` to a per-push resume position (sentence index). The two-tier ring is a change in `setUnreadIndicator` callers: pulse only when an unanswered ask exists.

· Cleaning: VF-43's sanitize step produces the listening text + span map {range, spokenAs}; stored message stays verbatim (messages.json untouched); the grown view renders spans italic+✦; ⌃⌥V flips views.
