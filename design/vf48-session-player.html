2 · Consumption — the two-tier ring, and what "consumed" means

The new dot grammar — NINE slots (⌃⌥1–9). 1 consumed (grey — when its session is also done it leaves the picker, freeing the number for the next queued session; numbers never shift while occupied), 2 active, 3 unread (static amber — no pulse), 4 asking (pulse reserved for questions). Everything that ever happened stays in the panel. Slack: bold for unread, red only for direct address. tmux: stable numbers.

The consumption rule (podcast three-state, extended to text): a push is consumed when its text was actually rendered in the grown view (Telegram viewport rule — a receipt flash never consumes) OR when TTS finished speaking it (~95%). Partial listening leaves an in-progress resume position. A consumed session's dot greys; once its session is also finished it leaves the picker and its number goes to the next queued session (numbers never shift while occupied). And because rendered = consumed, nothing auto-hides anymore (your call): a grown stack stays until you Esc/✕/switch — no more panel vanishing mid-read. Every stack stays browsable in the Agents panel forever (Visual Voicemail: the row never moves). A new push fully re-lights a session. Asks are NEVER consumed by reading or listening — they pulse until answered. Consumption is strictly user-local: agents never see "seen", only "delivered".

5 · Open decisions — needs your call (no product precedent)

1 · What consumes on display? — RESOLVED: rendered = consumed (Telegram viewport rule), and all auto-hide timers are REMOVED — a grown stack stays until you Esc/✕/switch, because the panel kept hiding mid-read.
2 · Does listening to the CLEANED text count as seeing the verbatim one? Recommend: yes for the unread ring (the meaning was delivered); the ✦ marks stay visible in the panel so you can always audit what was swapped.
3 · Cross-session auto-advance — RESOLVED: nothing happens at the end of a stack. End tone, done. No auto-advance, no "next" receipt — the end of a stack is where the user decides.
4 · Chord budget — RESOLVED (80/20): ⌃⌥,/. and ⌃⌥V are cut. Net new: one chord (⌃⌥0 last-session toggle) plus ⌃⌥7–9 extending the session family; F8 gains press-count semantics on the key it already owns.
5 · Now Playing registration — RESOLVED: Settings toggle, off by default.
6 · Slot aging — RESOLVED: NINE slots (⌃⌥1–9), not six. Numbers never shift while occupied; sessions beyond nine wait in a queue, and when a slot's session is consumed and finished, it leaves the picker and the next queued session takes the freed number. Everything remains in the panel forever.
7 · Progress representation — RESOLVED (your 12:56 capture): the hairline is gone everywhere; on the strip the waveform is the progress bar and the scrubber (lit = played, drag to seek), the label carries "2/3" for the stack; the collapsed pill shows no progress at all.
8 · Panel in-progress marker — better approach. Recommend: no graphics at all — express resume state as plain TEXT in the row's existing preview: the row reads builder — paused · 2/3 in the muted color. It respects remark #4 exactly (words, not decoration), reuses the strip's "2/3" vocabulary, and needs zero new UI. The graphical sliver from journey step 6 is withdrawn.

6 · Engineering notes (for the implementation ticket)

· Seekability: read-aloud of a stack starts from full known text → generate as cached audio (TTSController already caches finished streams and seeks cached WAVs; `seek(to:)` is cache-only today). Sentence boundaries → frame offsets gives exact sentence skips. Live streamed replies stay skip-limited (no cache) — the player shows pause/stop only there.

· Waveform: bars are the RMS amplitude envelope of the cached WAV (downsampled to ~16–24 buckets — exactly how WhatsApp draws voice notes), so the strip shows the real shape of the speech. Lit bars = played fraction; drag = `seek(to:)`; the 2 bars at the playhead animate while playing. Live streams draw the envelope of what has already been fed and mark the strip non-draggable.

· Speed: baked into generation per request. The chip steps ±0.1× and re-generates from the current sentence at the new speed — cheap because speech is already sentence-chunked (AgentReplySpeaker).

· State: SessionPush already carries seen/spoken/done — the three-state model extends `spoken` to a per-push resume position (sentence index). The two-tier ring is a change in `setUnreadIndicator` callers: pulse only when an unanswered ask exists. Auto-hide removal: the `autoHide` parameter of `showGrown`/`setTargetSession` goes away — collapse only on Esc/✕/click/switch. Session slots: register ⌃⌥7/8/9 (keycodes 26/28/25) alongside today's six; `pickerSessions()` gains the ≤9 queue with number retention.

· Cleaning: VF-43's sanitize step produces the listening text + span map {range, spokenAs}; stored message stays verbatim (messages.json untouched); the grown view renders spans italic+✦; ⌃⌥V flips views.
