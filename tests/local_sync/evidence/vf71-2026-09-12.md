# VF-71 — automatic phone sync

The September 8 task fixed the Mac firewall, stale address recovery, authenticated
rediscovery and sync merging. Those changes were present in this workspace and
installed on the Mac, but had not been committed. Its device check forced the
periodic job to run; that proved the job could sync, not that new captures or
network recovery would trigger delivery automatically.

## Established failure paths

- BubbleService retried only pending **audio** on network return/startup. Once a
  dictation was transcribed, an empty audio queue returned before any sync call,
  even if the finished transcript was still unsynced.
- Saving a local dictation or continuation did not schedule delivery. If its
  immediate in-process sync failed or never ran, it depended on the periodic
  job, which was registered only by MainActivity and could be deferred by Android.
- A shared `stopped` flag in the old JobService could let a stopped attempt
  finish a replacement attempt when a new start reset that flag.

The pre-fix Android build reproduced the first two gaps in the isolated QA app:
save a finished transcript without an Activity, then start the bubble with no
queued audio. Neither path uploaded it. The old diagnostics do not record enough
detail to attribute the user's specific September 12 incident completely; the
physical-phone follow-up below establishes its current state and the repair.

## Repair

Local dictation/chat writes now schedule persisted delivery immediately, with
expedited scheduling when available, a normal-job fallback, and exponential
retry starting at 30 seconds. New edits replace that job, preserving another
attempt when an edit races an upload. Each JobService run owns its completion;
stopped runs cannot finish a newer run.

Bubble startup/connectivity now syncs finished transcripts independently of its
audio queue, resets discovery cooldown on a new network, and ensures the
periodic catch-up job exists. Sync failure cannot interrupt transcript delivery.
Redacted diagnostics record the trigger, pending count, result and timing.
